ConTracktor_v1 1.6.7 - PAYROLL REOPEN AND REPLACEMENT
=====================================================

INSTALL ON THE CLIENT COMPUTER
1. Close ConTracktor completely.
2. Download ConTracktor_v1_Update_1.6.7_PayrollReopen.exe from the official
   GitHub v1.6.7 release.
3. Run the updater for the same Windows user who normally uses ConTracktor.
4. Open ConTracktor normally. Existing records remain in place.

AUTOMATIC SAFETY COPIES
- Database:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ConTracktor_v1\Data\Backups\Before_v1.6.7_<timestamp>\
- Previous application:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ConTracktor_v1\AppBackups\Before_v1.6.7_<timestamp>\

REOPEN A COMMITTED WEEKLY PAYROLL
1. Open Payroll > Committed Weekly Payrolls.
2. Select the payroll and click Reopen for Correction.
3. Enter the required correction reason.
4. Review the confirmation, including any linked payments that will be
   reversed, then authorize the action.
5. The original payroll and expense remain in history as Payroll Reopened.
6. The attendance returns to Weekly Payroll for the same Saturday-Friday week.
7. Correct attendance or final pay as needed.
8. Commit the week again. The new payroll and expense will show that they
   replace the original; the original changes to Superseded.

ACCOUNTING EFFECT
- The old payroll expense is excluded from current expense totals.
- Active payments on that expense are reversed, restoring their PC, DP, or
  bank balances.
- Salary deductions and payroll adjustments return to pending and are posted
  once against the replacement payroll.
- Original payroll totals and daily attendance remain available for audit and
  PDF export.

IMPORTANT
- Do not uninstall ConTracktor before updating.
- Do not replace or copy over contractor_tracker.db manually.
- Keep the installer and automatic backup until the updated records are
  reviewed.
- Download only from the official repository and compare the installer SHA-256
  with SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page.
