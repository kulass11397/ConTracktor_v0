ConTracktor_v1 1.6.6 - PAYMENT SOURCES AND PAYROLL PDF OPTIONS
================================================================

INSTALL ON THE CLIENT COMPUTER
1. Close ConTracktor completely.
2. Download ConTracktor_v1_Update_1.6.6_PaymentSources.exe.
3. Run the updater and confirm installation.
4. Open ConTracktor normally. Existing records remain in place.

AUTOMATIC SAFETY COPIES
- Database:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ConTracktor_v1\Data\Backups\Before_v1.6.6_<timestamp>\
- Previous application:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ConTracktor_v1\AppBackups\Before_v1.6.6_<timestamp>\

PAY ONE EXPENSE FROM MULTIPLE SOURCES
1. Open Expenses > Expense Ledger and select the expense or committed payroll.
2. Click Payments / Funding.
3. Click Add Payment Source and record the first PC, DP, or bank amount.
4. Repeat Add Payment Source for each additional source.
5. The window shows the running paid and outstanding totals.
6. If a source is insufficient, confirm the available partial amount and then
   add another source for the balance.

CORRECT A WRONG PC, DP, OR BANK SOURCE
1. Select the expense and click Payments / Funding.
2. Select the incorrect payment row and click Reassign Selected.
3. Choose the correct source, enter the correction reason, and authorize it.
4. The old and new allocation balances and the expense status recalculate
   automatically; the correction stays visible in the audit history.
5. If the old allocation has already been surrendered or returned, first open
   Petty Cash & Direct Procurement and void that cash return. Reassign the
   payment, then close/surrender the corrected allocation again if appropriate.

EXPORT A PAYROLL PDF
1. Open Payroll > Committed Weekly Payrolls.
2. Select a batch and click Export Selected Payroll PDF, or open the batch and
   click Export Payroll PDF.
3. Choose Summary Only or Full Ledger + Daily Attendance.
4. Select the save location.

IMPORTANT
- Do not uninstall ConTracktor.
- Do not manually replace contractor_tracker.db.
- Keep the installer and the automatic backup until the update is verified.
