ConTracktor_v1 1.6.3 - WITHDRAWAL FEE UPDATE
================================================

INSTALL ON THE CLIENT COMPUTER
1. Close ConTracktor completely.
2. Copy ConTracktor_v1_Update_1.6.3_WithdrawalFee.exe to the client computer.
3. Run the updater and click OK.
4. Open ConTracktor normally. Existing records are retained.

AUTOMATIC SAFETY COPIES
- Database:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ConTracktor_v1\Data\Backups\Before_v1.6.3_<timestamp>\
- Previous application files:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ConTracktor_v1\AppBackups\Before_v1.6.3_<timestamp>\

CORRECT THE P445,045 / P45 FEE / P161 SURRENDER CASE
1. Open Expenses > Petty Cash & Direct Procurement.
2. In cash-return history, select the P161 surrender and choose Void / Restore Cash Return.
3. If the P161 was already redeposited, void its redeposit first, then void the surrender.
4. Open Remittances and edit the original withdrawal.
5. Set Cash amount to 445,000.00 and Withdrawal fee to 45.00, then confirm.
6. Return to Petty Cash & Direct Procurement and close/surrender the allocation again.
7. The new surrender should be 116.00.

EXPECTED RESULT
- Physical cash received / allocation principal: P445,000.00
- Separate bank-fee expense: P45.00
- Total bank debit: P445,045.00
- Corrected cash surrender: P116.00

DO NOT copy or replace contractor_tracker.db manually during this update.
