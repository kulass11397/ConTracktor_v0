ConTracktor_v1 1.6.5 - BATCH DRAFTS AND STAGED-ENTRY EDITING
=============================================================

INSTALL ON THE CLIENT COMPUTER
1. Close ConTracktor completely.
2. Download ConTracktor_v1_Update_1.6.5_BatchDrafts.exe.
3. Run the updater and confirm installation.
4. Open ConTracktor normally. Existing records remain in place.

AUTOMATIC SAFETY COPIES
- Database:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ConTracktor_v1\Data\Backups\Before_v1.6.5_<timestamp>\
- Previous application:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\ConTracktor_v1\AppBackups\Before_v1.6.5_<timestamp>\

SAVE OR REOPEN A BATCH DRAFT
1. Open Batch Expenses or Batch Cash Advance.
2. Stage one or more entries and click Save Draft.
3. Later, open the same batch window and click Open Draft.
4. Select the saved draft and click Open Selected.
5. Saving a draft does not post expenses, payments, advances, or cash movement.

EDIT A STAGED CASH ADVANCE
1. Select one staged employee and click Edit Selected, or double-click the row.
2. Correct the amount, purpose, repayment method, or weekly deduction limit.
3. Change the shared Effective Advance Date if the whole batch date is wrong.
4. Click Apply Changes.

CANCEL DURING FINAL REVIEW
- If Confirm/Authorize is cancelled, the batch window reopens with all staged
  rows retained. Nothing is posted until the final approval succeeds.

EXPORT CASH ADVANCES
1. Open Payroll > Cash Advances.
2. Click Export Advances PDF.
3. Check one or more repayment methods, or click Select All.
4. Click Export PDF and choose where to save it.

CORRECT THE P445,045 / P45 FEE / P161 SURRENDER CASE
1. Open Expenses > Petty Cash & Direct Procurement.
2. Select the P161 surrender and choose Void / Restore Cash Return.
3. If it was redeposited, void the redeposit first, then void the surrender.
4. Open Remittances and edit the original withdrawal.
5. Set Cash amount to 445,000.00 and Withdrawal fee to 45.00.
6. Return to Petty Cash & Direct Procurement and close the allocation again.
7. The corrected surrender should be 116.00.

EXPECTED ACCOUNTING
- Physical cash / allocation principal: P445,000.00
- Linked bank-fee expense: P45.00
- Total bank debit: P445,045.00
- Corrected surrender: P116.00

Do not uninstall ConTracktor and do not manually replace contractor_tracker.db.
